A Letter From Jerome

Why We Are Doing This

LONG-FORM LETTER: NATHAN TO PROVIDE. Draft below is a placeholder in Jerome's voice.

Albuquerque gave me everything. I grew up here. I built my business here. My family is here.

And every year, I see families in this city who cannot put food on the table or a gift under the tree. That is not okay with me.

So on November 14th, we are doing something about it. We are bringing the bike community, the car clubs, the lowriders, and the exotics together for one run. Every car and every bike pays $10 to enter. 100% of that money goes to the kids.

I want this to be the biggest toy run Albuquerque has ever seen. If we do this, we do it right. Come ride with us.

Jerome Maldonado
Founder, Quad J Capital

Where Your Help Goes

The Charities We Support

[PHOTO: Roadrunner Food Bank site, food distribution]

Roadrunner Food Bank

Roadrunner Food Bank is the largest food bank in New Mexico. It gets food to families across the state through pantries, schools, and partner agencies.

Every can you bring to the run goes to them.

[STAT from their site]
[PHOTO: St. Martin's Hospitality Center site]

St. Martin's Hospitality Center

St. Martin's serves people in Albuquerque who are experiencing homelessness. It gives meals, housing help, and a path back to stability.

Your food donations help them keep the kitchen running this winter.

[STAT from their site]
[PHOTO: toy drop-off / kids with toys]

Toys For Families In Our Own City

Every toy stays in Albuquerque. We deliver them to local kids who would have nothing under the tree this year.

Bring one new, unwrapped toy. We handle the rest.
